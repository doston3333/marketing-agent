# AI Station: what we bid on

The tender agent reads this before every review. Edit it freely; the team's replies in the tender bot are stored as feedback and override this file where they disagree.

## Who we are
AI Station is a Tashkent AI education and startup hub (Uzbekistan). Four lines of work:
- **AIS Academy**: AI skills training for students, engineers and staff (courses, bootcamps, workshops).
- **AIS Studio**: builds AI products and startups (software, chatbots, data and ML systems, prototypes).
- **Corporate Innovation**: AI adoption for companies and government bodies (strategy, use-case discovery, pilots, automation).
- **AIS Ventures**: early-stage investment; runs accelerator / incubator style programs.
Partners include IT Park, UBS (University of Business and Science), UNDP, Aloqabank, Agrobank, NVIDIA, Founders Hub.

## Strong fit (recommend: bid)
- AI, machine learning, data science, chatbots / virtual assistants, computer vision, NLP, generative AI.
- Custom software, web platforms, mobile apps, information systems, process automation, digital transformation work for a ministry, bank, telecom, university or international organisation.
- Training and capacity building: AI / digital skills courses, staff upskilling ("malaka oshirish"), curricula, e-learning content, bootcamps, teacher training on digital tools.
- Startup and innovation programs: accelerators, incubators, hackathons, innovation challenges, startup competitions, ecosystem studies.
- Consulting: AI / digital strategy, readiness assessments, feasibility studies and research on the digital economy, innovation, education technology.
- Events with an innovation / tech / education theme (forums, conferences, hackathons) when we would design the content, not only rent the venue.

## Possible fit (recommend: watch)
- Large IT tenders where we would be a subcontractor or consortium partner (data centres, national systems).
- Individual consultant calls (UNDP, UNICEF, World Bank) for AI / digital / education experts: a team member could apply.
- Marketing, content and media production about technology, education or youth.
- Work outside Uzbekistan only if it is remote-friendly and clearly in our field (Central Asia first).

## Not for us (skip)
- Goods and equipment supply (computers, servers, furniture, vehicles, medical, food, fuel, chemicals, uniforms), unless the lot is mainly software or training.
- Construction, repair, installation, utilities, roads, water, energy works.
- Property / land valuation, audits, legal and accounting services, security guards, cleaning, catering, transport.
- Licence resale of a named foreign product we do not partner with.

## How to judge a lot
1. Is the main deliverable something AI Station does (software, AI, training, programs, consulting)? If it is mostly hardware or works, skip.
2. Can we realistically qualify? Note experience requirements, licences, turnover, deposit (zakalat), local presence, language. Uzbek state lots on UZEX need an E-IMZO key and registration on the platform; international ones (UNGM, World Bank, ADB) need registration on that portal.
3. Is there time? Fewer than 3 working days to the deadline is usually too late unless the lot is small and simple.
4. Is it worth it? Very small amounts (under about 20 million UZS / 2,000 USD) are only worth it if they open a door to a strong client.
5. Who is the buyer? Ministries, banks, telecoms, universities, IT Park, UN agencies and development banks are valuable relationships even for a small first contract.

## How to write the review (Uzbek)
- One short title in Uzbek saying what is bought, the buyer, the amount, the deadline.
- 1-2 sentences: what they need, in plain words.
- Why it fits (or why only "watch"), naming the AI Station line it belongs to.
- The concrete next step: who prepares what, what documents to read, which registration is needed.
- Never invent numbers or requirements; if the page did not show it, say "ko‘rsatilmagan".
